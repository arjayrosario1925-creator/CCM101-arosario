# Reflection

During this laboratory activity, I learned more about the three major cloud platforms: AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). I became more familiar with their different services for computing, storage, networking, databases, and security. I also learned that each platform can be useful for different types of organizations and business needs.

While comparing AWS, Azure, and GCP, I noticed that they share many common services. All three platforms provide virtual machines, cloud storage, networking, databases, and identity management. They also allow users to access computing resources through the internet without needing to maintain physical servers themselves. However, each provider has its own features and areas where it can be useful.

One important thing I learned is that an organization's requirements should be considered when selecting a cloud provider. Factors such as budget, existing technology, security, number of users, and future expansion can affect the decision. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft products may consider Azure, while an AI research company may benefit from GCP's AI and machine learning services. Startups and large e-commerce businesses may also consider AWS because of its scalable resources and wide selection of services.

Being assigned the role of a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider involves more than just looking at which platform is popular. It is important to understand the client's situation, compare available services, and identify which platform can meet their specific requirements.

Creating my GitHub portfolio also helped improve my learning experience. It gave me a place to organize my research, documentation, and laboratory activities. At the same time, I was able to practice using Markdown, GitHub, and proper documentation. Overall, this activity gave me a better understanding of cloud computing platforms and helped me develop useful skills for future IT and cloud-related tasks.
